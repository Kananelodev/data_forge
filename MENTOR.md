# Mentor Sessions

Each quest runs through the same four-step loop. It's how a fundamental goes
from "I've read about it" to "I own it."

```
 1. TEACH      plain idea first, then the proper terms
 2. BUILD      you make the gate green in solutions/
 3. EXPLAIN    you write journal/qXX.md in your own words
 4. INTERROGATE I ask the questions an interviewer or your BBD mentor would
```

Step 4 is where the real learning happens. It's meant to be a bit uncomfortable.

## Starting a session
Open a chat with Claude and say something like:

> "Mentor session — q03. Here's my journal entry: …"

or, for a fresh quest:

> "Mentor session — teach me q04."

Paste your journal entry and, if you're stuck, your solution and the failing
test output. Give the **quest id** so the session stays on track.

## Forging new gates
When you reach a ⚒️ forthcoming quest, the session forges its gate together:
we agree what "correct" means, write the test, then you build to it. Add the
new files and push — the quest flips from ⚒️ to 🟠 on the board.

## Boss quest (q17)
No tests. A full mock interview where you defend every design choice in
passport-pipeline out loud. Its journal entry is the write-up of how it went —
including what you got wrong.
